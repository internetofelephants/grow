# Timeline facts

As a run passes each marker, one of its four facts is picked at random and fades in above the trees,
e.g. "25 years ago, Wikipedia went online." Markers come every 25 years up to 400, then every 100 years
up to 3,000. Exact facts carry their year, so "N years ago" is counted from the current year (in 2027 the
Wikipedia fact says 26 years ago). From 500 years on the text says "About N years ago" and the facts are eras.

The facts live in `HISTORY` in main.js; this file is the reviewed copy. **Each was checked** (October 2026)
against Wikipedia articles, which give dates and cite their sources (not listed in References, by choice).
